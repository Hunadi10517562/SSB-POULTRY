About SSB Poultry

We are committed for sustainable farming, high quality poultry production and serving our local community with trust and great service.

Our Story & Mission

Founded with a vision to help and give out localized poultry supply, SSB Poultry specializes in raising healthy, high yield broiler chickens, collecting farm fresh daily eggs and supplying excellent nutritional feed. We bridge the gap between commercial grade farming standards and accessible, wholesome produce for local households and retail partners.

Our Core Values

Quality Assurance

We maintain a strict hygiene protocols, optimal housing temperatures and balanced nutrition to guarantee premium health across all our birds.

Sustainability

Our operations focus on sustainable agricultural practices that minimize environmental impact while optimizing feed efficiency and farm output.

Community Trust

We take pride in building long lasting relationships with local farmers, everyday consumers and commercial stockists through reliable service.
